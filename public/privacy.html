Privacy Notice

How we collect, use and protect your personal information. Last updated: October 2026.

Talent Placement & Support ("TPS", "we", "us") is a recruitment agency and employment business based in Northern Ireland. We find permanent staff for employers, and we supply temporary workers, mainly in hospitality and care.

TPS is the controller of the personal data described in this notice. This means we decide how and why it is used.

Address: The Mount Business Centre, 2 Woodstock Link, Belfast BT6 8DD, United Kingdom.

Email: enquiries@talentplacementsupport.com

1. Who this notice is for

This notice explains how we handle personal data about:

  • people looking for work through us (candidates and temporary workers)
  • people who work for our clients, and our other business contacts
  • referees whose details are given to us
  • anyone else who contacts us

2. What information we collect

Candidates and temporary workers

  • name, address, phone number and email address
  • CV, work history, skills, qualifications and training
  • the type of work, pay and location you are looking for
  • proof of identity and of your right to work in the UK
  • references
  • notes from our conversations and interviews with you
  • for temporary workers: National Insurance number, tax and bank details, timesheets and pay records

Sometimes we also need more sensitive information:

  • health information, so we can make reasonable adjustments or check that you can safely do a role
  • criminal record information from an AccessNI check, where the law or the role requires it (for example, care work)
  • equal opportunities information, but only if you choose to give it

If you do not give us the information we need, we may not be able to find you work or supply you to a client.

Clients and business contacts

  • name, job title, work email address and phone number
  • details of the jobs you ask us to fill
  • invoice and payment records

3. Where we get your information

  • from you, for example when you send us your CV, apply for a job or speak to us
  • from job boards and professional networking sites, where you have made your profile available to recruiters
  • from referees and past employers, with your permission
  • from our clients, for example interview feedback
  • from AccessNI, if a check is needed
  • from our payroll provider, for temporary workers

4. How we use your information and our legal reasons

  • Finding you work and introducing you to clients. We do this because it is in our legitimate interests as a recruitment business and in your interests as a job seeker. It is also needed to take steps you have asked for before entering a contract. We will always ask you before we send your CV to a client.
  • Supplying and paying temporary workers. This is needed to carry out our contracts with you and with our clients.
  • Checking identity, right to work and suitability, and keeping records. The law requires this, including immigration law, tax law and the Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005.
  • Managing our relationship with clients, sending invoices and collecting payment. This is needed to carry out our contracts and for our legitimate interests in running our business.
  • Telling you about new jobs or our services. We do this for our legitimate interests. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • Dealing with complaints and legal claims. We do this for our legitimate interests and where the law requires it.

Sensitive information

We only use health or criminal record information when we need it to meet our legal duties relating to employment, or when you have given your clear consent. We keep it secure and only share it when we have to.

5. Who we share your information with

We never sell your personal data. We only share it when needed, with:

  • clients who are considering you for a job (only with your permission)
  • referees, to confirm your references
  • AccessNI, for criminal record checks
  • our payroll provider, HMRC and pension providers, for temporary workers
  • companies that provide our IT, email, data storage and recruitment software
  • our accountants, insurers and legal advisers
  • the police, regulators (such as the Employment Agency Inspectorate) or courts, when the law requires it

6. Sending information outside the UK

We aim to keep your information in the UK. If one of our service providers stores information outside the UK, we make sure it is protected in line with UK data protection law. For example, we use countries the UK recognises as having adequate protection, or approved contract terms.

7. How long we keep your information

  • Candidate details: up to 2 years after we last had contact with you, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Records about placements, temporary work, pay and invoices: up to 6 years, for tax and legal reasons.
  • AccessNI certificate information: no longer than necessary, in line with the AccessNI Code of Practice.

After these periods, we delete your information securely.

8. Keeping your information safe

We keep personal data on password-protected systems and limit access to people who need it for their work. We ask the companies that handle data for us to keep it secure as well.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • ask us to delete your information
  • ask us to limit how we use your information
  • object to how we use your information, including for marketing
  • ask us to send your information to you or another organisation in a format that is easy to use
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on your consent

To use any of these rights, email enquiries@talentplacementsupport.com. There is usually no charge. We will reply within one month. Sometimes the law requires us to keep certain information, and if so we will explain why.

We do not make decisions about you using only automated means.

10. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator:

  • website: ico.org.uk
  • phone: 0303 123 1113

11. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, and is available from us on request.
