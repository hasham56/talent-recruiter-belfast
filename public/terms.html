Part A — General

1. Who these terms are between

1.1 These terms are between Talent Placement & Support ("TPS", "we", "us") and the business that asks us to find or supply staff ("the Client", "you").

1.2 In these terms, "the Client" includes any company in the same group as the Client, and any business run by the same directors or owners. It also includes any business the Client passes a Candidate's details to.

1.3 For permanent placements, TPS acts as an employment agency. For temporary and contract workers, TPS acts as an employment business. Both terms have the meaning given in the Employment (Miscellaneous Provisions) (Northern Ireland) Order 1981.

2. Meaning of key words

  • Candidate — a person TPS introduces to the Client for a permanent role.
  • Temporary Worker — a person TPS supplies to work for the Client on a temporary or contract basis. TPS, or a payroll provider TPS uses, employs or engages them.
  • Introduction — any of the following, which then leads to the person being Engaged:
    • TPS giving the Client a CV or other details that identify a person
    • the Client interviewing a person TPS has put forward, in person, by phone or by video
  • Engagement / Engaged — the Client taking a person on in any way: employment, contract for services, freelance, through another agency, or otherwise. It does not matter whether the work is permanent, fixed-term, full-time or part-time.
  • Introduction Fee — the fee due under Part B when a Candidate is Engaged.
  • Assignment — a period during which a Temporary Worker works for the Client.
  • Charges — the hourly or daily rates the Client pays TPS for a Temporary Worker under Part C.
  • Remuneration — the gross annual pay of the Engaged person in their first year. It includes the items listed in clause 5.3.
  • Working Day — Monday to Friday, excluding bank and public holidays in Northern Ireland.

3. When these terms apply

3.1 TPS will send these terms to the Client before providing any service. The Client is treated as having accepted them as soon as the Client does any of the following:

  • gives TPS a vacancy to work on
  • asks to see or receives a CV
  • asks to interview a person TPS has put forward
  • accepts the supply of a Temporary Worker
  • signs these terms

3.2 These terms replace any terms the Client puts forward, including purchase order terms. The only exception is where a TPS director agrees otherwise in writing.

3.3 Any change to these terms must be agreed in writing and signed by a TPS director.

4. What the Client agrees to do

4.1 Give TPS accurate details of each role before we start work. This includes:

  • job title and duties
  • location
  • hours
  • pay and benefits
  • start date
  • any qualifications, licences, checks or experience required by law or by a professional body

4.2 Tell TPS in writing, before arranging an interview, if the role details change.

4.3 Tell TPS in writing within [number — to confirm] Working Days of receiving a person's details if the Client already has that person's details from another source. If the Client does not, the Introduction is treated as made by TPS.

4.4 Give TPS feedback on CVs and interviews within a reasonable time, so Candidates are kept informed.

4.5 Pay at least the National Minimum Wage or National Living Wage for the role, and comply with employment, health and safety and equality law.

4.6 Not discriminate against any person TPS introduces or supplies on any ground prohibited by law.

Part C — Temporary and contract workers

10. How temporary supply works

10.1 TPS supplies Temporary Workers to work under the Client's supervision and direction. TPS, or its payroll provider, pays the Temporary Worker and handles their PAYE, National Insurance, holiday pay, pension auto-enrolment and statutory payments.

10.2 Before each Assignment, TPS will confirm in writing:

  • the Temporary Worker's identity and relevant experience and qualifications
  • the start date and expected length of the Assignment
  • the Charges

10.3 The Temporary Worker is not the Client's employee. The Client must not change their pay, hours or duties directly. Any change goes through TPS.

11. Charges and timesheets

11.1 The Charges are [how rates are set — to confirm].

11.2 Higher rates apply for [overtime, nights, weekends, bank holidays and the uplift — to confirm].

11.3 The Client must sign or approve the Temporary Worker's timesheet each [week or fortnight — to confirm]. If the Client does not, TPS may invoice on the hours the Temporary Worker reports.

11.4 TPS will invoice [weekly or fortnightly — to confirm]. Invoices are payable within [number — to confirm] days.

11.5 TPS may change the Charges if the Temporary Worker's pay must rise by law. Examples include increases to the National Minimum Wage, or equal treatment under clause 13. TPS will give the Client reasonable notice of any such change.

12. Client duties during an Assignment

The Client must:

  • give the Temporary Worker a proper induction, supervision, and health and safety information, training and equipment
  • treat the Temporary Worker as it would its own staff for health and safety purposes, and report any accident to TPS straight away
  • not ask the Temporary Worker to do work outside the agreed role, or work that needs a licence or qualification they do not hold
  • have suitable insurance in place, including employers' and public liability cover, for work done under its control

13. Agency Workers Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2011

13.1 From day one, the Client must give Temporary Workers:

  • access to shared facilities such as a canteen, childcare or transport
  • information about job vacancies, on the same basis as its own staff

13.2 After 12 weeks in the same role, Temporary Workers are entitled to the same basic pay and working conditions as if the Client had hired them directly.

13.3 The Client must give TPS, on request and promptly, accurate details of the pay and conditions of comparable staff. The Client must also tell TPS about any earlier Assignments the Temporary Worker has done for the Client through other agencies.

13.4 TPS may increase the Charges to reflect equal treatment under clause 13.2.

14.1 If the Client reasonably finds a Temporary Worker unsuitable, it should tell TPS in writing within [number of hours — to confirm] hours of the start of the Assignment. In that case TPS will [what TPS does — to confirm].

14.2 If TPS receives information suggesting a Temporary Worker may be unsuitable, it will tell the Client and act as the Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2005 require. This may include ending the supply.

14.3 Either party may end an Assignment by giving [notice period — to confirm] notice. TPS may end an Assignment at once if the Client breaches these terms or the Temporary Worker's safety is at risk.

15. Taking on a Temporary Worker directly (temp-to-perm)

15.1 A transfer fee is payable if, during the Relevant Period, the Client does any of the following with a Temporary Worker:

  • employs or Engages them directly
  • uses them through another agency or employment business
  • introduces them to another business that Engages them

15.2 The Relevant Period ends on whichever of these dates is later:

  • 14 weeks after the first day the Temporary Worker worked for the Client in that Assignment. Any work before a break of more than 42 days is ignored.
  • 8 weeks after the last day the Temporary Worker worked for the Client.

15.3 The transfer fee is [fee — to confirm] plus VAT.

15.4 Extended hire option. Instead of paying the transfer fee, the Client may tell TPS in writing that it wants an extended hire period of [number of weeks — to confirm] weeks. During that period TPS will supply the Temporary Worker on terms no less favourable than those that applied just before the notice. At the end of the extended hire period, the Client may take the Temporary Worker on without paying a transfer fee.

15.5 If TPS cannot supply the Temporary Worker for the whole extended hire period, the transfer fee is not payable. This does not apply if the failure was in no way TPS's fault.

15.6 No transfer fee is payable if the Engagement starts after the end of the Relevant Period.

Part D — General terms

16. Payment, VAT and late payment

16.1 All fees and Charges are subject to VAT at the current rate.

16.2 If an invoice is paid late, TPS may claim interest and fixed compensation under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998. Interest is charged at 8% a year above the Bank of England base rate. TPS may also claim reasonable recovery costs.

16.3 The Client may not withhold or set off payment against any claim it has against TPS.

16.4 Any rebate under clause 8 is only available if the invoice was paid on time.

17. Checks and references

17.1 TPS takes reasonable steps to confirm a person's identity and that they have the experience, training and qualifications the Client says it needs. TPS also checks that they are willing to do the work.

17.2 For permanent Candidates, the Client is the employer and must carry out its own right-to-work check before the Candidate starts. The Client must also take up any references, AccessNI or DBS checks, or other checks it needs.

17.3 For Temporary Workers, TPS carries out the right-to-work check unless agreed otherwise in writing.

17.4 TPS cannot guarantee that references will be returned. It can only contact a person's current employer with that person's consent.

18. Confidentiality and data protection

18.1 Information about Candidates and Temporary Workers is confidential. The Client may use it only to consider them for the role. The Client must not pass it to anyone else without TPS's written consent.

18.2 Both parties must comply with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. The Client must keep personal data secure, and delete it once it no longer needs it for the recruitment process.

18.3 The Client must not contact a person's current or past employers for a reference without that person's consent.

18.4 TPS's privacy notice explains how TPS handles personal data: [link to privacy notice — to confirm].

19. Liability

19.1 TPS takes reasonable care in finding and supplying suitable people. However, the Client is responsible for deciding whether a person is suitable to employ or use.

19.2 TPS is not liable for any loss, damage, cost or delay the Client suffers because of:

  • a person TPS introduces or supplies, including their conduct, negligence or dishonesty
  • a person not attending, not accepting a role, or leaving
  • TPS being unable to introduce or supply anyone

19.3 TPS's total liability under these terms is limited to [liability cap — to confirm].

19.4 Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot be limited by law.

19.5 The Client will cover TPS for any loss TPS suffers because the Client breaches these terms or the law. This includes health and safety failures, discrimination, and breaches of the Agency Workers Regulations.

20. Advertising

20.1 TPS may advertise the Client's vacancies on its website, on job boards and on social media without naming the Client. The exception is where the Client asks in writing for a role to be kept confidential.

21. Other terms

21.2 If any part of these terms is found to be unenforceable, the rest still applies.

21.3 A delay in enforcing a right does not mean TPS has given up that right.
